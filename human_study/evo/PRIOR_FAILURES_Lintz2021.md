# Lessons from the lab's previous cage (Lintz M, PhD dissertation, Cornell 2021, Bonassar / Haertl; PI upload 2026-10-26)

Chapter 4 "In vivo assessment of biodegradable support structures for TDR in the minipig cervical spine" (PLA, FDM-printed,
100 % infill, C3/4 and C5/6, n = 4, 4 weeks, empty cages):
| Observation | Consequence for evo |
|---|---|
| Two-piece cage: disc height index -63.7 % and complete collapse at week 1 (halves slid apart / separated) | one-piece insertion only (already S2); any multi-part concept must prove the parts cannot separate |
| One-piece cage stayed in place 4 weeks but DHI fell 13.6 % (C3/4) and 39.7 % (C5/6) | height maintenance over time (S7) must be judged over the support period, not only at implantation |
| EVERY cage fractured at the same two posterior sites, independent of design and level; cause: a posterior bony process of the endplate concentrating repeated load (geometry, not material) | evaluate on the real endplate geometry with contact (our FE model does); add cyclic / fatigue check of peak stress at contact hot spots, especially posterior; uncinate processes / posterior lip are the human analogue |
| Uniform compression test of the cage alone: 134 MPa without failure, ~70x the cervical IDP -> misleading | never accept a design on a uniform platen test; L1 / L2 must load through the bones (already planned) |
| PLA softened during 37 C culture (hydrolysis) | material properties must be wet 37 C (hard PLCL already 37 C wet) and degrade over the support period |
| Canine study (ch. 1, Moriguchi 2017): constructs displaced at certain levels (C5/6 ~100 %) from curvature / loading; resorbable plate restored > 25 % stiffness and prevented extrusion (Mojica-Santiago 2018) | slip-out is the known failure mode (main goal S9); plate is outside our space rule (strictly in the disc space) |
| TE-IVD integration and native-like stiffness reached in vivo after 6 weeks - 9 months (rat), 16 weeks (dog), 8 weeks (goat); engineered disc ~10x softer than native before implantation | support period: design for >= 16 weeks (sensitivity 26 weeks); biodisc gives ~no load support at implantation (consistent with dropping it from the screening model) |
