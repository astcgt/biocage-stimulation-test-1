# Legends for the friction lower bound figures (scripts h45, h46)

**fig_mu_poster | The friction needed to stop the whole cage being squeezed out is below the friction assumed available.** mu_req = mu_squeeze of e04, computed on the glued (tied) run at 1.5 N m under a 100 N follower load: the least friction coefficient at which both faces together can carry the net force and twist on the cage. It answers whether the whole cage can be squeezed out, not how far it moves. Friction assumed available: 0.3, range 0.2-0.45 (PI 2026-10-26), unmeasured. **d**, largest absolute change of mu_req across motions; time step, contact stiffness and mesh on p062 only, softening on 5 designs, GPU vs CPU on 15 windows with byte-identical inputs. Relative changes reach 7.7 % where mu_req is very small.

**fig_mu_gaps | What the same number cannot yet tell us.** **a**, tension on the glued face over its net normal force, worst face per window. **b**, mu_scaled of e03 on each face (includes the twist the face carries), worst face, against mu_req; values above 1 are not reachable by friction, which indicates the glued load path would not exist in a real contact. **c**, share of the 79 six-motion designs that hold at an assumed friction. **d**, cage translation over its limit at the gate-4 evaluation point; squares and circles physio_fric3, triangles early runs physio_fric and physio_fric2. **e**, FEBio drift at a fixed angle (results/gpufe_verify/v2/v17a_hold.json, hold from t 3.9). DECISIONS 2026-11-06 00:50 quotes the same runs from a common start at t 3.91: p062 +0.047 and +0.303 mm, p139 +0.167 and +0.461 mm. **f**, gaps and what would close them.

Per-face values come from a glued solution, so they are an estimate of demand, not a measured slip. The statement that the real need lies between the two measures is a hypothesis, not a test result. All friction verdicts remain provisional. The qa-auditor agent has not reviewed these figures.
